# Data Story — BigBasket Category Performance

## Which categories are ahead of target

Three of six categories are beating their monthly revenue targets across Jan–Jun 2026:

- **Bakery** — ₹15,410 vs. a ₹12,000 target, **+28.4%** above target
- **Household Essentials** — ₹21,715 vs. a ₹17,000 target, **+27.7%** above target
- **Personal Care** — ₹16,382 vs. a ₹15,500 target, **+5.7%** above target

Household Essentials is both the single largest revenue category overall and comfortably ahead of its target — it's the strongest performer in the catalog.

## Which categories are behind target

Three categories are falling short:

- **Dairy & Eggs** — ₹14,090 vs. a ₹16,500 target, **-14.6%** (Below Target - Watch)
- **Snacks & Beverages** — ₹10,895 vs. a ₹13,000 target, **-16.2%** (Below Target - Critical)
- **Fruits & Vegetables** — ₹9,790 vs. a ₹12,000 target, **-18.4%** (Below Target - Critical)

Fruits & Vegetables has both the lowest absolute revenue (₹9,790) and the largest percentage shortfall of any category, making it the clearest underperformer.

## Recommendations

**1. Invest catalog and marketing effort in Fruits & Vegetables.** It has the largest percentage shortfall (-18.4%) and the lowest total revenue of any category. Given fruits and vegetables are typically high-frequency repeat purchases for grocery shoppers, a targeted push — expanded SKU selection, better placement in the app, or a promotional campaign — could meaningfully close this gap.

**2. Review Snacks & Beverages' supplier and catalog mix.** This category is also in "Below Target - Critical" territory (-16.2%) despite having the highest order *count* (83 orders) of any category — more orders than Household Essentials (79) or Personal Care (66), yet lower total revenue than either. This points to a low average order value in this category specifically, suggesting the product mix skews toward lower-priced items. The category team should review whether pricing, bundling, or promoting higher-margin SKUs within Snacks & Beverages could lift revenue per order.
